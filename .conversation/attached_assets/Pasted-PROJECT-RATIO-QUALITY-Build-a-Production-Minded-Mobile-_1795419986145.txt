PROJECT: RATIO QUALITY

Build a Production-Minded Mobile-First Quality Inspection PWA — Functional Prototype

You are a senior full-stack engineer, product designer, mobile UX specialist, and software architect.

Build a fully functional, Arabic-first, mobile-first Progressive Web App called Ratio Quality | ريشيو للجودة.

This application will be used by coffee shop quality inspectors to conduct branch inspections, document findings with photos, record measurements, evaluate compliance, and generate professional inspection reports directly from their smartphones.

The application should combine the best workflow concepts found in modern inspection platforms such as SafetyCulture, Mitti, Joynt, GoAudits, and Lumiform, without copying their proprietary interfaces or branding.

IMPORTANT: Build a real, working prototype. Not a landing page, not a static UI, and not a collection of disconnected mockups.

1. PRODUCT VISION

The primary experience must be extremely simple:

1. Open the application from the iPhone home screen.
2. Select a branch.
3. Start an inspection.
4. Read one question.
5. Select an answer.
6. Take a photo if necessary.
7. Write or dictate a short observation.
8. Continue to the next question.
9. Complete the inspection.
10. Review results and generate a professional Arabic PDF report.

An inspector should not need to navigate complex menus while standing behind a coffee bar.

Prioritize speed, clarity, reliability, and minimal interaction.

12. WEIGHTED SCORING ENGINE

Implement an independent scoring module.

Severity weights:

* Critical = 10
* Major = 5
* Minor = 1

Formula:

Weighted Compliance = (Sum of compliant applicable question weights / Sum of all applicable question weights) × 100.

Exclude Not Applicable questions from the denominator.

Classification:

* 90% or higher: ممتاز
* 80% to below 90%: جيد
* 70% to below 80%: يحتاج تحسين
* Below 70%: غير مقبول

Critical failure rule:

If any critical question is non-compliant, the final classification must automatically become “غير مقبول” regardless of the weighted score.

Keep the calculated score visible separately from the overridden classification.

Do not calculate a final score if required questions are unanswered.

Include unit tests for the scoring logic.
